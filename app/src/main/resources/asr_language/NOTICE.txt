Shona Voice uses a table of Shona character 1–5-gram counts to decode MMS
frame scores. This adaptation is licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

Sources:
1) Leipzig Corpora Collection, Shona Web subcorpus (Zimbabwe), 2018,
   sna-zw_web_2018_100K, 100,000 sentences, CC BY-NC 4.0.
   Persistent identifier: https://hdl.handle.net/11022/0000-0007-CA65-4
   Catalog: https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/Shona
   Archive: https://downloads.wortschatz-leipzig.de/corpora/sna-zw_web_2018_100K.tar.gz
2) FLEURS: Few-shot Learning Evaluation of Universal Representations of
   Speech (2022), Alexis Conneau, Min Ma, Simran Khanuja, Yu Zhang,
   Vera Axelrod, Siddharth Dalmia, Jason Riesa, Clara Rivera and Ankur Bapna.
   Shona training subset, sn_zw, revision 70bb2e84b976b7e960aa89f1c648e09c59f894dd.
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs
   The original FLEURS data are licensed under CC BY 4.0.
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: normalized and deduplicated each source; counted contiguous
character sequences including spaces; weighted FLEURS training counts
by four and pruned infrequent longer sequences. No evaluation transcripts
were included. Rebuild with tools/build_shona_prior.py.
